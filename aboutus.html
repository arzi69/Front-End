About Us

EL Nurado is a fashion brand that prides itself on creating stylish and high-quality products. Our mission is to offer timeless fashion that transcends trends. We combine modern design with classic aesthetics to create pieces that are both stylish and wearable.

Founded in 1975 in Northumbria, we are committed to bringing sustainable, ethical fashion to the world. Our materials are sourced responsibly, and we prioritize fair labor practices throughout our production process.

At EL Nurado, we believe that fashion should be inclusive, empowering, and expressive. Our collections are designed for individuals who value craftsmanship and uniqueness. We are constantly evolving, staying ahead of the curve with fresh styles and innovative designs.

Meet Our CEO

Our CEO, Lado EL Nurado, is a visionary leader with over 25 years of experience in the fashion industry. Under Lado EL Nurado's leadership, EL Nurado has become a benchmark for innovation, sustainability, and timeless style.

Key Achievements:

  • Founded the company in 1975 and transformed it into a global fashion brand.
  • Implemented sustainable production practices that set industry standards.
  • Won multiple awards for innovation, design, and ethical business practices.
  • Mentored the next generation of designers and industry leaders.

With a passion for excellence and a commitment to social responsibility, Lado EL Nurado continues to drive the brand forward, ensuring that every piece of fashion not only looks good but also supports a better, more sustainable future.
